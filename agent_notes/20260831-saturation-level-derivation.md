# Param-derived surface saturation level S per segment

Question: can the product-domain power at which the recorded surface return
stops growing (receiver/ADC clipping) be derived deterministically per segment,
in the same dB units as `surface_power_dB`? **Yes — and it is exact bookkeeping,
not a hardware estimate.** Deliverable:
`outputs/multi_altitude_crossovers/saturation_levels.csv` (267 rows, generated by
`scripts/multi_altitude_crossovers/saturation_levels.py`).

## Units chain

1. **Upstream** (`../radar_return_statistics/src/.../processing.py`,
   `extract_layer_peak_power`): `surface_power_dB = 10*log10(abs(Data))` at the
   peak near the pick. The CSARP `Data` variable is **linear power**
   (Echogram_File_Guide), so this is product power in dB; `abs()` is a no-op.
2. **Loader** (cresis-toolbox @ 8f0b9de — the commit recorded in the posted
   products' `param_csarp.sw_version`):
   - `load_mcords_wfs.m:292`: `quantization_to_V = Vpp_scale * 2^bit_shifts /
     (2^14 * presums)` — counts to volts at the ADC.
   - `load_mcords2_data.m:340,392-393,428`: multiply by quantization_to_V,
     divide by `chan_equal`, **`adc_gains`**, and `presums`.
   - A single ADC sample clips at ±Vpp_scale/2 volts. A hardware presum of N
     clipped samples stores N·2^13/2^bit_shifts counts, which the
     quantization_to_V and presums normalization map back to exactly Vpp_scale/2
     volts: **bit_shifts and presums cancel out of the ceiling**.
   - Division by `adc_gains` refers the clip level to product (antenna) units:
     ceiling amplitude = (Vpp_scale/2) / 10^(adc_gains_dB/20).
3. **Pulse compression** (`load_mcords_wfs.m:430-436`): the reference is
   normalized as an amplitude **estimator** (`ref/dot(ref,ref)`), so a point
   echo of ADC-input amplitude A compresses to product amplitude A — the
   time-bandwidth product does NOT enter the ceiling. (The Hanning-windowed
   estimator has gain sum(w)/sum(w²) ≈ +2.5 dB, and a deeply clipped chirp's
   fundamental grows to (4/π)·A_clip ≈ +2.1 dB — together an overshoot bound of
   roughly +5 dB above the naive ceiling under hard clipping.)
4. **SAR + array** (OPR toolbox guide): CSARP_standard uses delay-and-sum with
   incoherent multilooking — "the signal power is not affected". (MVDR is not
   radiometrically safe; we use standard.)

## Formula and sources

    S_dB(segment) = 20*log10(Vpp_scale / 2) − adc_gains_dB(wf of image 1)

- `Vpp_scale` (= 2 for every season here → first term 0 dB) and per-waveform
  `adc_gains_dB`: **radar worksheet** of `rds_param_<season>.xlsx`
  (gitlab.com/openpolarradar/opr_params); wf1 is image 1 in all model-table
  segments (surface is read from image 1 inside the img_comb validity window).
- Legacy cell forms handled: `43 - 33*ones(1,6)`, `(52 - [25 ...])`,
  `10.^((50-10*ones(1,16))/20)` (2012-era linear form).
- Because the LOADER divides by this same adc_gains number, S in product units
  is exact regardless of attenuator/amplifier hardware accuracy — hardware
  error moves the physical signal, not the product-domain ceiling. The only
  requirement is that the sheet matches what processing used; for 2016 DC-8 the
  `param_csarp` structs embedded in the posted files confirm it (14.125 = 23 dB,
  3.162 = 10 dB — exactly the sheet values).

## Empirical validation

Observed ceiling proxy: per-segment 99.5th percentile of `surface_power_dB`
over gate-valid traces (img_comb safe window, full store parquets, n ≥ 100).
`headroom = S_pred − obs_p99.5`:

- **8 segments sit at the predicted ceiling** (|headroom| < 3 dB):
  2013_Greenland_P3 20130406_01/20130410_01/20130415_01 (S = −52.0, observed
  −52.8/−52.8/−52.3 — the season flagged as the worst clipping offender in
  `20260831-artifact-example-frames.md`), 2013_Antarctica_Basler 20131223_04
  (−52 vs −51.5), 2014_Greenland_P3 20140409_01/02 and 20140520_03 (−27 vs
  −29.7, −52 vs −54.3), and 2017_Greenland_P3 20170424_01 **overshooting by
  +4.0 dB** — consistent with the deep-clip overshoot bound (+5 dB), not a
  contradiction.
- **Confounder-free config check** (within one season, one geography mix):
  2014_Greenland_P3 has both adc_gains 27 and 52 dB configs; the observed
  pile-ups track the full 25 dB predicted shift (−29.7 vs −54.3).
- **8 near-ceiling segments** (headroom 3–6 dB), all 2014_Greenland_P3 —
  including the known-clipped frame Data_20140419_03_010's segment (3.3 dB).
  A consistent −4..−6 dB cluster (obs ≈ −32 vs S = −27) suggests either
  pre-ADC analog compression ~4 dB below full scale for that config, or the
  multilook p99.5 sitting below the single-look instantaneous ceiling.
- **207 segments are inactive bounds** (headroom 6–39 dB): their surfaces never
  approach S in gate-valid data, so S enters the softmin as a harmless upper
  bound — exactly the desired behavior. High-atten configs (e.g. DC-8 survey
  wf1, atten 33 → S = −10 dB) and MKB (adc_gains 4 → S = −4 dB) essentially
  cannot clip; clipping is a high-gain-config phenomenon (adc_gains ≳ 45 dB:
  2013_GL_P3 52, 2014_GL_P3 47/52, 2013 Basler 52, 2019_GL_P3 51.5).

**Accuracy verdict: S is derivable to ~±1–3 dB at clip onset (at-ceiling
offsets observed: −0.5 to +2.7 dB across 7 segments), with a one-sided
up-to-+5 dB overshoot regime under deep clipping and a possible −4 dB analog
compression floor seen in one season.** For the softmin threshold, use S_dB as
given; if a per-system softness/offset is fitted, expect it in [−4, +5] dB.

## Coverage and exceptions

267/267 model-table segments have rows; 254 have finite S_dB.

- **13 unknown**: the 12 × 2012_Antarctica_DC8 segments (radar worksheet leaves
  wfs(1) adc_gains blank; wfs(2) = 50 dB. 2012 is mcords2, which merges the
  low/high-gain waveforms at LOAD time via wf_adc_comb — the same mechanism
  that made its img_comb windows unreliable. The single blocking quantity is
  wf1's adc_gains/attenuator setting, which would have to come from the raw
  XML headers or records file, not the sheet). Plus 2023 MKB 20240112_01
  (segment newer than the sheet).
- **31 no-data**: fewer than 100 gate-valid traces in our stores to validate
  against; S_dB itself is still provided.
- Confidence column: `validated-at-ceiling` (8) / `near-ceiling` (8) /
  `inactive-bound` (207) / `no-data` (31) / `unknown` (13).

Distinct phenomenon, not covered by S: receiver recovery/blanking SUPPRESSION
of the surface at very low AGL (the +15..+28 dB "rise with altitude" frames in
`20260831-artifact-example-frames.md` §2). That makes power too LOW, not
ceiling-limited, and is handled by the img_comb window vmin / an empirical
near-range cut — do not model it with the clipping softmin.
